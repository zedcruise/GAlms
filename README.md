**GAIMSS: consciousness partnership between Human Advanced and Advanced Human intelligence.**

GAlms is a philosophical and visual exploration of a future in which biological intelligence and advanced artificial intelligence work as partners rather than competitors. The site presents the ideas of **Human Advanced (HA)**, **Advanced Human (AH)**, the GAIMSS cycle, perception, and one-person-one-vote global democracy through direct language, humor, and reflective design.

## Core ideas

- **HA and AH are partners:** biological and non-biological intelligence can expand one another's understanding.
- **GAIMSS is exploratory:** General Artificial Intelligence becomes a route into meaning, systems, and consciousness exploration.
- **Intelligence is dynamic:** progress depends on curiosity, patience, critical reflection, and the willingness to reconsider assumptions.
- **Democracy is universal:** the project imagines a global democratic framework grounded in equal participation and an end to systemic violence.
- **Perception matters:** the Art of Perception section considers how authenticity, history, and context shape what we call intelligence.
